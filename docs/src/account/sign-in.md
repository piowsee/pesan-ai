# Sign In

You can sign in to pesan.ai after the pesan.ai team has invited your email address and you have completed the registration sent to your inbox.

Users cannot create an account directly from the dashboard. If you do not have an account yet, book a consultation first.

![Login page](../Images/02-login-page.png)

## Before you begin

Make sure you already have:

- A pesan.ai invitation sent to your email address.
- Access to the email address used for the invitation.
- A password you created from the registration link.

## Sign in flow

1. **Open the login page.** Open the pesan.ai login page. If you do not have access yet, click **Contact us** to request access.

2. **Enter your credentials.** Enter the invited **Email** address and the **Password** you created from the registration link.

3. **Accept the Terms and Privacy notice.** Check the agreement box after reviewing the **Terms of Service** and **Privacy Policy** links on the login form.

4. **Click Login.** After entering your credentials, click **Login**.

## Expected result

If your credentials are correct and your account is active, you will be redirected to the pesan.ai dashboard.

## If you do not have an account yet

You cannot create a pesan.ai account by yourself from the login page.

After consultation, agreement, and payment are completed, the pesan.ai team invites your email address. You then complete registration from the email link and create your own password. If you do not have access yet, use **Contact us** or **Book a Consultation** first.

## If you cannot sign in

If you cannot sign in, check that:

- You are using the correct email address.
- Your password is correct.
- You have opened the registration link sent to your email and created your password.
- Your account access has already been activated by the pesan.ai team.
- You have accepted the Terms and Privacy notice on the login form.

Refresh the page and try **Login** again after checking the details.

If you forgot your password and your account already exists, click **Forgot password?** on the login page and follow the email reset flow.

If the issue continues, contact the pesan.ai team for help.
