# Glossary

## Pesan AI

A managed WhatsApp AI automation platform that connects WhatsApp Business to a web dashboard and supports AI-assisted customer communication.

---

## WhatsApp Business Account / WABA

A WhatsApp Business Account is the business asset used to manage WhatsApp Business numbers through Meta's business tools and platform.

---

## WhatsApp Business App

The mobile app used by businesses to communicate with customers through WhatsApp.

---

## WhatsApp Business Platform

Meta's platform for businesses that want to connect WhatsApp communication to external systems and tools.

---

## Business Portfolio

A Meta business asset container used to manage business accounts, permissions, and related assets.

---

## Coexistence

A setup option that allows an existing WhatsApp Business App account to connect to the WhatsApp Business Platform while still using the app.

---

## AI Agent

An AI-assisted component that can help handle repetitive customer messages based on agreed business flows when automation is enabled for the service.

---

## Take over

A Chat action where a human team member handles a conversation manually.

---

## Return to bot

A Chat action that allows the bot or AI-assisted workflow to resume handling new messages when that service flow is enabled.

---

## Customer contact

A customer number or contact record collected from interactions with connected WhatsApp Business Accounts.

---

## Export Excel

A feature for downloading customer contact data from the Customers page as an Excel file.
