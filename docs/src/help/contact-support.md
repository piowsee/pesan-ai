## When to contact us

Contact the pesan.ai team directly if:

- You cannot sign in or complete registration.
- A WABA or WhatsApp number does not appear after onboarding.
- You see an unexpected WABA status or connection issue.
- You selected the wrong chat history sharing option during coexistence setup.
- You are not sure which setup option to choose.

When possible, include your business name, login email, WhatsApp number, and a short description of the issue.
