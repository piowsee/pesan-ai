# Troubleshooting

This page lists common issues that may happen while using pesan.ai.

## I do not have an account

pesan.ai accounts are created by the pesan.ai team.

Book a consultation first, then wait for the team to contact you and complete the onboarding process.

---

## I cannot sign in

Check that:

- You are using the correct email address.
- Your password is correct.
- Your account has already been created and activated.
- You have accepted the Terms and Privacy notice on the login form.

If the issue continues, contact the pesan.ai team.

---

## The Meta popup does not open

Try the following:

- Allow popups in your browser.
- Check your internet connection.
- Try again from WABA Management.
- Use a supported desktop browser.
- Make sure you are opening pesan.ai from an HTTPS URL.

---

## I selected the wrong business portfolio

Restart the onboarding process from WABA Management or contact the pesan.ai team for help.

---

## I did not receive a Facebook Business message in WhatsApp

Check that:

- You entered the correct WhatsApp Business phone number.
- The phone has an active internet connection.
- You can open the WhatsApp Business App.
- You are checking the correct WhatsApp account.

Then retry the connection from WABA Management.

---

## I shared chat history by mistake

Contact the pesan.ai team.

pesan.ai currently does not support chat history sync, so users should choose **Don't share chats** during the coexistence setup.

---

## Connected account shows Suspended or needs attention

Open WABA Management and review the account status.

If you are not sure what action is required, contact the pesan.ai team.

---

## Export Excel does not download

Try the following:

- Check whether filters are active.
- Confirm there are customer rows to export.
- Allow downloads in your browser.
- Retry the export.
- Contact the pesan.ai team if the issue continues.
