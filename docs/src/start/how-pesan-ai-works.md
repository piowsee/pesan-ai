# How pesan.ai works

pesan.ai works as an end-to-end managed service for WhatsApp Business communication.

Instead of asking users to configure everything by themselves, the pesan.ai team guides clients from consultation to email invitation, account setup, WhatsApp Business connection, and service activation.

## Overall flow

1. You book a free consultation.
2. The pesan.ai team contacts you through WhatsApp or email.
3. You discuss your business needs, WhatsApp setup, customer communication flow, and AI automation goals.
4. After the agreement and payment process are completed, the pesan.ai team invites your email address.
5. You open the registration link from your email and create your password.
6. You sign in to the pesan.ai dashboard.
7. You connect your WhatsApp Business Account through WABA Management.
8. Your WhatsApp channel becomes available inside pesan.ai.
9. Your team can monitor conversations, review customers, and use the platform as the control panel for WhatsApp-based customer communication.
10. When AI automation is enabled for your service, the AI Agent can help handle repetitive customer messages based on the agreed business flow.

## Why consultation is required

Consultation is required because every business may have a different WhatsApp setup, customer journey, automation need, team structure, and service scope.

During consultation, the pesan.ai team can help confirm:

- Which WhatsApp number should be connected.
- Whether you already use WhatsApp Business App.
- Whether coexistence setup is needed.
- What kind of customer messages should be handled.
- Whether AI-assisted replies are needed.
- When human attention is required.
- What data or contact export needs should be prepared.

## What happens after setup

After you complete registration and your WhatsApp Business Account is connected, you can use the dashboard to review account status, open WABA Management, monitor Chat once available for your service, and review customer contacts from the Customers page.
