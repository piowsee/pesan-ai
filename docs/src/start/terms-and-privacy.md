# Terms of Service and Privacy Policy

Before using pesan.ai, you can review the Terms of Service and Privacy Policy from the login page or legal pages.

These pages explain the rules for using the service and how your information is handled.

## Terms of Service

The Terms of Service explain the rules, responsibilities, limitations, and service usage conditions for pesan.ai.

Read this page to understand what is allowed, what the service covers, and what responsibilities apply when using the platform.

## Privacy Policy

The Privacy Policy explains how pesan.ai handles information related to your account, business, contacts, and service usage.

Read this page before using the dashboard or connecting your WhatsApp Business Account.

## When to read these pages

You should read these pages:

- Before using the dashboard.
- Before connecting a WhatsApp Business Account.
- Before giving pesan.ai access to business assets through Meta onboarding.
- Whenever you need to understand how the service handles business and contact information.
