# Check WABA Status

After connecting a WhatsApp Business Account, you can check its status from the WABA Management page.

## Where to check status

Open **WABA Management** from the sidebar.

The page shows a summary of total accounts, active accounts, and accounts that need attention.

![WABA Management account list](../Images/04-waba-management-page.png)

## Status types

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Active | The account is connected and ready to use. |
| Suspended | The account may be suspended or blocked, or may need action before it can be used normally. |
| Needs attention | A dashboard summary label for accounts that require action or review. |
| Not connected or incomplete | The setup has not been completed or the account is not connected. |

## Connected numbers

Each account row may show one or more connected WhatsApp numbers.

Use the number dropdown to review the numbers connected to the account. If needed, use **Add number** from the number menu.

## What to do if an account needs attention

If an account shows **Suspended** or appears under **needs attention**, the connected account may require review or action.

Check that:

- Meta onboarding was completed.
- The correct WhatsApp number was connected.
- The Meta business asset is still accessible.
- The WhatsApp Business Account is active.

If you are not sure what action is required, contact the pesan.ai team.
