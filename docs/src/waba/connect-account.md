# Connect a WhatsApp Business Account

To use pesan.ai with WhatsApp, you need to connect a WhatsApp Business Account from the WABA Management page.

This process opens Meta onboarding through Facebook Login for Business. Meta may update the screens from time to time, but the purpose remains the same: to grant pesan.ai access to the correct WhatsApp Business assets.

## Before you begin

Make sure you have:

- Access to the Facebook account that manages the business assets.
- Access to the correct business portfolio.
- Access to the WhatsApp Business number you want to connect.
- Your phone nearby if you are connecting an existing WhatsApp Business App account.

## Connection flow

1. **Open WABA Management.** From the sidebar, click **WABA Management**.

2. **Click Connect WhatsApp account.** Click **Connect WhatsApp account** to open Meta onboarding.

3. **Continue in the Meta onboarding popup.** A Facebook Login for Business popup will appear. Click **Continue** to start the onboarding process.

![Meta onboarding popup](../Images/05-meta-onboarding-popup.png)

4. **Select or confirm business assets.** Follow the Meta onboarding steps to select your business portfolio and WhatsApp Business setup option.

![Select business portfolio](../Images/06-business-portfolio-selection.png)

5. **Choose a setup option.** During the onboarding process, you may choose one of the following options:

| Option | Description |
|---|---|
| Use an existing WhatsApp Business App account | Use coexistence mode to connect an existing WhatsApp Business App account. |
| Create a new WhatsApp Business Account | Create a new WhatsApp Business Account through the Meta onboarding flow. |

![Choose existing or new WhatsApp setup](../Images/07-existing-or-new-waba-choice.png)

## If you are not sure which option to choose

Choose **Use an existing WhatsApp Business App account** if your business already uses WhatsApp Business App and wants to keep using the same number.

Choose **Create a new WhatsApp Business Account** if your business wants to create a new WhatsApp setup through Meta.

If you are not sure, contact the pesan.ai team before continuing.

## If the Meta popup does not open

Allow popups in your browser, check your internet connection, refresh the page, and try **Connect WhatsApp account** again from **WABA Management**.

Facebook Embedded Signup requires an HTTPS page. If the button is disabled or the popup still does not open, contact the pesan.ai team.

## Expected result

After the setup is completed, return to pesan.ai and open WABA Management. Your connected WhatsApp Business Account should appear in the account list.
