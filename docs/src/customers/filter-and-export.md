# Filter and Export Contacts

The Customers page allows you to filter customer contacts and export the result.

![Customers page filters and export](../Images/18-customers-page.png)

## Filter and export flow

1. **Open Customers.** From the sidebar, click **Customers**.

2. **Filter by WABA.** Use the **All WABAs** dropdown to show customers from a specific WhatsApp Business Account. Inside the filter, you can choose **Select all** or select individual WABAs.

3. **Filter by number.** Use the **All numbers** dropdown to show customers from a specific connected WhatsApp number. Inside the filter, you can choose **Select all** or select individual numbers.

4. **Clear filters.** Click **Clear** to remove active filters and return to the full list.

5. **Export to Excel.** Click **Export Excel** to download the customer contact list.

The exported file can be used for follow-up, reporting, or operational review. The export includes customer name, phone number, and a WhatsApp chat link when available.

## If Export Excel does not download

Check your browser download permission and Downloads folder. If filters are active, the export follows the selected **All WABAs** and **All numbers** filters.

Refresh the page, click **Clear** to remove filters if needed, and try **Export Excel** again. If the issue continues, contact the pesan.ai team.

## Notes

- If filters are active, the exported data follows the selected filters.
- If there are no customer rows, the export button is disabled.
- If the download does not start, check your browser download permission and try again.
